Harmonized dataset from the Brazilian National Survey of Basic Sanitation (PNSB) 2000 by IBGE

This dataset provides the municipal tables of the Pesquisa Nacional de Saneamento Básico (PNSB) 2000, published by the Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) in its SIDRA system: 143 tables on water supply, sewage, solid waste, storm water and municipal management, one row per municipality (5,507, DTB edition 2000), with 2,285 value columns.

Every table has the same key, municipio_code (7 digits, text), so it joins to the municipal meshes of the Brazilian Territorial Division (DTB) and to the other IBGE datasets of the same year, such as the 2000 census. Simplified plotting layers from the DTB are included.

What is in this deposit

  • tables IBGE_PNSB-2000_TABELAS_BRASIL_2000.zip — 143 CSV files, one per SIDRA table (IBGE_PNSB-2000_T<table>_BRASIL_2000.csv): water supply (38), sewage (26), solid waste (46), storm water (29) and management (4).
  • plotting geography IBGE_PNSB-2000_GEO_BRASIL_2000.zip — GeoPackages of municipalities, seats, states and the outline of Brazil.
  • database T2_IBGE_PNSB-2000_BD_BRASIL_2000.gpkg.zip — one GeoPackage built from the files above, described below.
  • metadata catalogs: LAYERS (every file, Portuguese and English), FIELDS, VARIABLES (every value column: SIDRA variable, unit, classifications and categories), TABLES (every SIDRA table: name, subject, period, rows), CONSISTENCY (uncompressed, readable on the repository).
  • checksums: IBGE_PNSB-2000_MANIFEST_X_X.csv and IBGE_PNSB-2000_SHA256SUMS_X_X.txt.

The CSV files use ; as separator and UTF-8 encoding.

Database

The GeoPackage IBGE_PNSB-2000_BD_BRASIL_2000.gpkg has one table per SIDRA table (t<table>, same columns and values as the CSV, typed: codes and names text, values integer or real, unique index on municipio_code), the plotting layers with geometry, and the catalogs, with a layers table describing all of them.

Conventions

- Columns are named v<variable>_<category> with the SIDRA ids; their meaning is in VARIABLES.
- SIDRA symbols: - → 0, X (value hidden) → -1, .. and ... → -9999 (no data). Text fields use NA.
- Rows are the 5,507 municipalities of the DTB edition 2000 (SIDRA lists today's municipalities for every year; the later ones are dropped). Nine tables cover only part of them; the others get -9999, listed in CONSISTENCY.
- 32 tables span several survey years in SIDRA (2000–2008, 2000–2017); this deposit keeps their 2000 values (the period is in TABLES).

Plotting geography

The layers (ibge_dtb_municipios_2000, ibge_dtb_sedes_2000, ibge_dtb_unidades_2000, ibge_dtb_pais_2000) come from the DTB 2000 mesh. They are simplified for maps (about 110 m, shared borders kept) and are not the official mesh.
