Harmonized dataset from the Population Estimates by IBGE

This dataset provides a harmonized version of the official municipal population series of Brazil published by the Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE): the yearly population estimates of every municipality from 1992 to 2026, together with the census populations of every municipality from 1872 to 2022.

The IBGE estimates are published as one spreadsheet (or PDF table) per year, each with its own layout, code format, footnotes and spelling of names. Here every year is read into the same table structure, municipality codes are completed to the current 7-digit format, footnotes are kept in a separate table, and each fix applied to the source is recorded in a consistency catalog. The files are distributed per year (one CSV per table and year, grouped in one zip per table), with a ready-to-use database that joins them in a few tables and fills the years without an estimate by interpolation, flagged in its own column.

Municipality codes are those of the year, so every table joins by municipio_code and year to the municipal meshes of the Brazilian Territorial Division (DTB) of the same year.

What is in this deposit

Files:

  • census populations IBGE_POP_CENSOS-MUNICIPIOS_BRASIL_1872-2022.zip — 12 files, one per census (1872, 1900, 1920, 1940, 1950, 1960, 1970, 1980, 1991, 2000, 2010, 2022), 38,372 rows: total population, urban and rural (from 1940), free and enslaved (1872).
  • municipal estimates IBGE_POP_ESTIMATIVAS-MUNICIPIOS_BRASIL_1992-2026.zip — 30 files, one per year, 164,398 rows: the resident population on 1 July of every municipality, its name as published and the footnote mark of the cell.
  • published totals IBGE_POP_ESTIMATIVAS-UNIDADES_BRASIL_1992-2026.zip — 22 files: the totals of Brazil, the regions and the states printed in the estimate files, for the years that publish them.
  • footnotes IBGE_POP_ESTIMATIVAS-NOTAS_BRASIL_1992-2026.zip — 27 files: the footnotes of every estimate file (court-ordered populations, corrections, name changes, sources).
  • database T2_IBGE_POP_BD_BRASIL_1872-2026.gpkg.zip — a GeoPackage (attribute tables, no geometry) built from the files above, described below.
  • metadata catalogs: IBGE_POP_LAYERS_X_X.csv, IBGE_POP_FIELDS_X_X.csv, IBGE_POP_CONSISTENCY_X_X.csv (uncompressed, readable on the repository).
  • checksums: IBGE_POP_MANIFEST_X_X.csv and IBGE_POP_SHA256SUMS_X_X.txt — size and SHA-256 of every file and of every CSV inside the zips.

Files are kept separate per year on purpose: a new estimate adds one file, and new versions of the deposit only replace the files that changed. The CSV files use ; as separator and UTF-8 encoding. All files of one table have the same columns, including year, so they can simply be concatenated.

Database

The GeoPackage IBGE_POP_BD_BRASIL_1872-2026.gpkg merges the per-year files into six tables:

- municipios — censuses, estimates and interpolated years in one long table (218,878 rows), unique on year + municipio_code + source.
- unidades — the published totals of Brazil, regions and states (719 rows).
- notas — the footnotes (128 rows).
- layers, fields, consistency — the metadata catalogs.

Three columns of municipios document where each value comes from:

- source: censo or estimativa for values as published by IBGE; interpolado, interpolado_territorio or interpolado_rateio for interpolated values.
- series: 1 marks the one row per year and municipality that forms the annual series 1872–2026 — the census when the year has one, otherwise the estimate, otherwise the interpolated value (213,371 rows). Other rows have 0, for example the 2000 estimate, which coexists with the 2000 census.
- interpolation: for interpolated rows, the method, the two years used and the municipalities added (e.g. geometric 2006-2008; 2008 + 2206720); NA otherwise.

Interpolated years

IBGE published no municipal estimate for 1996 and 2007 (years of population counts) nor for 2023. In the database these years are interpolated geometrically between the nearest years of the series (1995–1997, 2006–2008, 2022–2024), on the territory of the earlier year:

- interpolado (15,627 rows): the municipality is unchanged between the two years.
- interpolado_territorio (345 rows): the municipality lost territory to new municipalities created between the two years; their later populations are added to its own, so that both ends refer to the same territory.
- interpolado_rateio (136 rows): a new municipality was created from several municipalities; the whole group is interpolated together and the result is shared by the population of each in the earlier year.

The parent municipalities of each new municipality are taken from the DTB genealogy (IBGE). National totals of the series: 1996 157,502,411 (between 155,822,440 in 1995 and 159,636,413 in 1997), 2007 188,025,422, 2023 207,766,704. Urban and rural populations and the state totals are not interpolated. Interpolated values are a convenience for continuous series; they are not IBGE figures and are always marked by source.

Conventions

- Identifiers: municipio_code is text with 7 digits (including the check digit), as valid in that year. Municipalities that ceased to exist before the current coding have codes of the form XX999NN. unidade_code is the 2-digit state code.
- Population columns are integers. -9999 means no data (the column does not exist for that source or year, or the source has no value); text fields use NA. In the source, - is zero and ... is not available.
- Urban and rural populations exist only for the censuses from 1940 on: the censuses of 1872–1920 did not classify the population by situation, and the yearly estimates publish only the total.

Data quality notes

- Estimates 1992–2006 give 6-digit codes without check digit; they were completed with the code of the DTB edition of the year.
- Footnote marks written inside value or name cells (e.g. 59.881(*)) were separated into note_mark; their texts are in the footnotes table.
- Census populations 1872–2010 come from the IBGE table of the “Evolução da divisão territorial do Brasil 1872–2010”, whose codes match the DTB meshes of each year. Its 2010 column had thousand separators read as decimals (e.g. 24.392 for 24,392); every value was reconstructed so that total = urban + rural, and the national total equals the published 190,755,799. The 1960 values are the preliminary synopsis (IBGE note).
- The 2022 census populations are the sums of the census tracts (variable V0001 of the 2022 tract tables), urban and rural by tract situation; total 203,080,756, equal to the published figure.
- Names are kept as published; the 260 cases where they differ from the DTB name of the year are listed in the consistency catalog, together with the census values not available (28), the two 1940/1950 cases where total ≠ urban + rural in the source, and the six years (2009, 2012–2016) where the published total of Pará differs from the sum of its municipalities (both are kept).
- The estimates of 1975 and 1989 (scanned PDF tables) are not included.
