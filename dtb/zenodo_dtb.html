Harmonized dataset from the Brazilian Territorial Division (DTB) by IBGE, 1872–2022

This dataset provides a harmonized historical version of the Brazilian Territorial Division (Divisão Territorial Brasileira, DTB) published by the Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE): the municipal meshes, municipal seats and state boundaries of 14 snapshots from 1872 to 2022, the official lists of municipalities, districts and subdistricts of every DTB edition from 1940 to 2022, the IBGE municipal genealogy, and a splitting graph that links the municipalities of consecutive snapshots.

The IBGE sources come in many formats, code systems and spellings across 150 years. Here every snapshot has the same layer structure, the 7-digit municipality code of its year, consistent names and geometry types, and a single coordinate system (SIRGAS 2000, EPSG:4674). Every fix applied to the sources is recorded in a consistency catalog. The codes of each year are the ones used by the IBGE censuses and population estimates of that year, so census and population tables join to these meshes by municipio_code and year.

What is in this deposit

Files:

  • municipal meshes IBGE_DTB_MUNICIPIOS_BRASIL_1872-2022.zip — 14 GeoPackages, one per snapshot (1872, 1900, 1911, 1920, 1933, 1940, 1950, 1960, 1970, 1980, 1991, 2000, 2010, 2022), from 641 polygons in 1872 to 5,572 in 2022.
  • municipal seats IBGE_DTB_SEDES_BRASIL_1872-2022.zip — 14 GeoPackages of points, one per snapshot, with the federal and state capitals flagged (1872–1991).
  • states IBGE_DTB_UNIDADES_BRASIL_1872-2022.zip — 14 GeoPackages of the federative units (provinces in 1872) of each snapshot.
  • 2022 regional layers *_2022.gpkg.gz — Brazil (pais), major regions (regioes), mesoregions and microregions, intermediate and immediate geographic regions.
  • DTB lists IBGE_DTB_QUADRO-MUNICIPIOS_BRASIL_1940-2022.csv.gz (27 editions: 1940, 1950, 1960, 1970, 1980, 1994, 2000, 2003–2022, with the regional hierarchy), IBGE_DTB_QUADRO-DISTRITOS_BRASIL_1994-2022.csv.gz and IBGE_DTB_QUADRO-SUBDISTRITOS_BRASIL_1994-2022.csv.gz (19 editions).
  • genealogy IBGE_DTB_GENEALOGIA_BRASIL_1872-2010.csv.gz — the IBGE table “Municípios, genealogia e alteração de nome e grafia” in long form (40,610 rows, 5,592 municipal trajectories).
  • splitting graph IBGE_DTB_LINHAGEM_BRASIL_1872-2022.csv.gz — 41,792 edges between the municipalities of consecutive snapshots.
  • database T2_IBGE_DTB_BD_BRASIL_1872-2022.gpkg.zip — one GeoPackage built from the files above, described below.
  • metadata catalogs: IBGE_DTB_LAYERS_X_X.csv, IBGE_DTB_FIELDS_X_X.csv, IBGE_DTB_CONSISTENCY_X_X.csv (uncompressed, readable on the repository).
  • checksums: IBGE_DTB_MANIFEST_X_X.csv and IBGE_DTB_SHA256SUMS_X_X.txt — size and SHA-256 of every file and of every GeoPackage inside the zips.

Files are kept separate on purpose, so that new versions of the deposit only replace the files that changed. The CSV files use ; as separator and UTF-8 encoding and can be read compressed by pandas, pyarrow, DuckDB, R and most other tools; the .gpkg.gz files and the zips must be decompressed before use in GIS software.

Database

The GeoPackage IBGE_DTB_BD_BRASIL_1872-2022.gpkg has 56 layers, named without dataset:

- Geospatial, one layer per snapshot year: municipios_1872 … municipios_2022, sedes_1872 … sedes_2022, unidades_1872 … unidades_2022 (14 each).
- Geospatial, 2022: pais, regioes, mesorregioes, microrregioes, regioesintermediarias, regioesimediatas.
- Attribute tables: quadro_municipios, quadro_distritos, quadro_subdistritos (key edition + code), genealogia, linhagem.
- Three metadata layers: layers (description of every layer, Portuguese and English), fields (every column, with type and no-data value) and consistency (every fix applied to the sources).

Values and geometries are identical to the per-year files; the database adds a unique index on the code of every layer, indexes on the tables, and the GeoPackage spatial indexes.

Snapshots and sources

- 1872–1991: the meshes of the IBGE “Evolução da divisão territorial do Brasil 1872–2010”. IBGE built them by joining 1991 polygons, so these eleven snapshots nest exactly on the same cartographic base.
- 2000: the 1:500,000 municipal mesh; 2010: the 1:250,000 mesh; 2022: the 2022 municipal mesh. These are independent cartographic bases, so boundaries between them also differ for cartographic reasons.
- Seats 1872–2000 come with the meshes; 2010 and 2022 are the seats of the 2022 census, without the 5 municipalities installed in 2013 for 2010.
- States 2000 and 2010 are dissolved from the municipalities.

Splitting graph

Each edge of linhagem links a municipality of one snapshot to a municipality of the next, from the overlay of the two meshes, with the intersection area and the share of each end. Since IBGE never reuses a code, the codes drive the relation: continuation (35,064), split (5,510), transfer of territory between continuing municipalities (836), absorption (171), recode (116, e.g. Goiás → Tocantins in 1988), redistribution (78) and emergence (17, new codes born in disputed areas or outside the previous mesh). Check columns compare each edge with the interior point of the polygons and with the IBGE genealogy: the main parent agrees with the genealogy for 97–100% of the new municipalities in every interval. same_base tells whether both snapshots share the cartographic base, to filter the transfers that are only cartographic.

Conventions

- Identifiers: every column ending in _code is text. municipio_code has 7 digits including the check digit, as valid in that year; municipalities that ceased to exist before the current coding have codes of the form XX999NN.
- area_km2 is computed in an equal-area projection (ESRI:102033); in linhagem it is the intersection area.
- Special areas are kept in the municipal meshes so that every snapshot covers the whole country: disputed areas (MG/ES, PI/CE; 1940–1991), lagoons (Lagoa dos Patos and Lagoa Mirim, 1991–2022) and the unassigned islands of 1991. They are flagged by area_type (litigio, lagoa, ilha_nao_atribuida; municipio otherwise); the disputed areas and islands have no municipality code.
- -9999 means no data in numeric fields, NA in text fields (documented in fields).

Data quality notes

- Names follow the spelling of the time when a contemporary source has it: for 1940–1980 the DTB list of the year, then the genealogy, then the mesh; for 1920 the mesh (it keeps the 1920 spelling, e.g. Barcellos, Coary).
- Codes were fixed where the sources disagree with each other (21 mesh codes, 20 seat codes), e.g. swapped labels in the 1872–1991 base (Espírito Santo do Pinhal ↔ Santo Antônio do Jardim, Amélia Rodrigues ↔ Conceição do Jacuípe) and the two Entre Rios of 1920. 38 seats without code (São Paulo, 1970) took the code of the polygon containing them.
- No geometry was invented: municipalities listed by the genealogy without a polygon in the mesh are logged and kept out (e.g. Villa Franca 1872, Jatahy 1900–1920).
- 2000: three enclaves not cut from the surrounding polygon in the source (Ladário, Portelândia, Águas de São Pedro) were subtracted. The 2000 mesh has no projection file; it is labelled EPSG:4674 without transformation (the datum is likely SAD69, about 60 m, below the accuracy of a 1:500,000 mesh).
- 2010: 309 km² of the Maciço de Baturité (CE) belong to no municipality in the source and stay as holes.
- Genealogy: the municipality names in the notes (“desmembrado de …”) were resolved to codes; 20 remain unresolved and 2 ambiguous (logged). 91 codes of the DTB lists do not follow the IBGE check-digit rule and are kept as published.
